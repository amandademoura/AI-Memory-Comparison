# Announcement: Team Nova Leadership
Posted: January 8, 2024 — Priya Nair, Engineering Director
 
I'm glad to share that Alice Chen has agreed to take on the role of Lead for Team Nova, our perception and computer vision group. Alice joined Solvane in late 2023 after several years building vision pipelines for industrial robotics, and has already made a strong impression leading the calibration work on the S1 arm's camera stack.
 
Please join me in congratulating Alice as she takes the reins of Team Nova going forward.
