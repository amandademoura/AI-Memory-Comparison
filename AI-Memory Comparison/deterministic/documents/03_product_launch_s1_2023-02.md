# Product Spec Sheet: Solvane S1 Warehouse Picking Arm
Version 1.0 — Released February 14, 2023
 
**Overview:** The Solvane S1 is a six-axis robotic arm designed for pick-and-place operations in warehouse fulfillment environments.
 
**Specifications:**
- Reach: 1.2 m
- Repeatability: ±0.1 mm
- Maximum payload: 5 kg
- Power: 220V, 1.8 kW peak draw
- Vision system: Solvane Vision Suite (integrated)

The S1 is Solvane's first commercial product and has been validated across three pilot deployments with fulfillment partners.
