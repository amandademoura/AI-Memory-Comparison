# Vision Suite Pricing Update
Posted: January 15, 2025 — Solvane Product & Pricing Team

Starting today, we are moving Solvane Vision Suite to per-seat pricing: **$150/seat/month.**

This change **discontinues the flat-rate individual tier** ($499/month) introduced in 2023.
Existing customers on the flat-rate plan will be transitioned at their next renewal date; new customers signing up from today onward will be on the per-seat model only.
