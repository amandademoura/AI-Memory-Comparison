# Solvane Robotics — Employee Handbook (v1)
Effective: January 1, 2023
 
*Note: Solvane Robotics is a fictional company created for internal evaluation purposes.*
 
## 1. About Solvane Robotics
Solvane Robotics designs warehouse automation hardware and the software that runs it. We are organized into four engineering teams: Team Nova (perception and computer vision), Team Atlas (navigation and motion planning), Team Forge (hardware and manufacturing), and Team Helix (firmware).

## 4. Working Arrangements
### 4.2 Remote Work Policy
Solvane Robotics is a fully remote company. Employees are not required to work from any office location, and the company does not maintain mandatory in-office days. Teams are encouraged to meet in person periodically at their own discretion, but this is optional.
 
## 7. Expenses
### 7.1 Meals
Employee meals while traveling on company business are reimbursable up to $50 per day. Receipts are required for any single expense over $25.
