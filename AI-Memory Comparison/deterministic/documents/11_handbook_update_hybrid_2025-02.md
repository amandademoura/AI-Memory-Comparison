# Employee Handbook Update — Section 4.2: Working Arrangements
Effective: February 1, 2025

This update **supersedes Section 4.2 of the 2023 edition** of the employee handbook.

Solvane Robotics is moving to a hybrid working model. Employees are expected to work from an assigned Solvane office three days per week: Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday. Monday and Friday remain remote-flexible for all teams. Fully remote arrangements are still available by manager exception for roles hired as remote-only.
