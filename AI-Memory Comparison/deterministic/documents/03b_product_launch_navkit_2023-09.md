# Product Spec Sheet: Solvane Nav Kit
Version 1.0 — Released September 1, 2023
 
**Overview:** The Solvane Nav Kit is an autonomous mobile robot (AMR) platform for intra-facility
material transport, built on navigation stack developed by Team Atlas.
 
**Specifications:**
- Max speed: 1.5 m/s
- Payload capacity: 150 kg
- **Battery life: 8 hours of continuous operation in standard operating mode**
- Charging time: 90 minutes (fast charge)
- Navigation: LIDAR + visual SLAM, developed by Team Atlas under Marcus Webb
Standard operating mode assumes typical warehouse duty cycles with mixed idle and transit time.
