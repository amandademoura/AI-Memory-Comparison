# Product Spec Sheet: Solvane S1 Warehouse Picking Arm
Version 2.0 (Hardware Revision) — Released June 3, 2024

**Overview:** Version 2 of the S1 introduces a redesigned actuator assembly and updated gripper, improving load handling over the original 2023 release.

**Specifications:**
- Reach: 1.2 m (unchanged)
- Repeatability: ±0.08 mm (improved)
- Maximum payload: 7 kg
- Power: 230V, 2.1 kW peak draw

This revision replaces the actuator design from v1.0. All new units shipping from June 2024 onward are v2 hardware.
