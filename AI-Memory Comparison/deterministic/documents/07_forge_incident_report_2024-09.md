# Incident Review Summary: S1 Payload Field Reports
Posted: September 16, 2024 — Derek Osei, on behalf of the Team Forge review committee

Following several field reports of actuator strain on S1 v2 units operating near the published 7 kg payload limit, Team Forge conducted an internal review of the v2 hardware validation process (see the related errata notice for the corrected payload figure).

As part of the outcome of this review, leadership of Team Forge is transitioning. **Derek Osei is now Lead of Team Forge**, effective today. Sofia Kappler, Team Forge's founding lead, is moving into a newly created Quality Systems Advisor role, where she will focus on hardware validation processes across all product lines.

We thank Sofia for her work building Team Forge from the ground up, and are confident this transition strengthens our validation rigor going forward.
