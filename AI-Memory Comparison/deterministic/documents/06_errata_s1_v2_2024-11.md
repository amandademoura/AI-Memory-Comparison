# Errata Notice: Solvane S1 v2 Payload Rating
Posted: November 20, 2024 — Solvane Product Engineering

We are issuing a correction to the Solvane S1 v2.0 spec sheet (published June 3, 2024).

The 7 kg maximum payload figure published in that spec sheet was measured under controlled lab conditions only (fixed mounting, single-axis load). Under real-world deployment conditions with standard mounting hardware, the validated and rated maximum payload for S1 v2 hardware is 6 kg, not 7 kg.

**This corrects the June 2024 v2 spec sheet only.** 
It does not affect the original 2023 v1.0 spec sheet, which remains rated at 5 kg. Customers with v2 hardware should update their integration limits accordingly. A revised spec sheet (v2.1) reflecting the 6 kg rating will be published to the product portal.
