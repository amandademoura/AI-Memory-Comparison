# Engineering Org Update
Posted: March 3, 2025 — Priya Nair, Engineering Director

As part of this quarter's engineering reorganization, I want to share a leadership change on Team Nova.

Effective today, **Bob Okafor succeeds Alice Chen as Lead of Team Nova.** Bob has been a senior engineer on the team since mid-2024 and has been the primary driver behind the latest perception model improvements shipped in the S1's vision stack.

Alice Chen is moving into a Principal Engineer role, an individual-contributor position with no direct reports, where she'll focus on cross-team technical strategy for perception systems across both the S1 and future product lines. This change supersedes the leadership listed in earlier announcements — Bob, not Alice, is the point of contact for Team Nova going forward.

Thank you both for the smooth transition.
