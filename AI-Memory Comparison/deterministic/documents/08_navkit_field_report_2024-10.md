# Field Report: Nav Kit Battery Performance
Posted: October 1, 2024 — Team Atlas Field Engineering

We've collected battery performance data from Nav Kit units running in "eco mode," a lower-power navigation profile introduced in a firmware update earlier this year.

In eco mode, customers are seeing **up to 10 hours of continuous operation** on a single charge, compared to the standard 8-hour rating published at launch. Eco mode reduces LIDAR scan frequency and caps max speed at 1.0 m/s, which is why runtime improves.

To be clear, this does not change the standard-mode battery rating — 8 hours remains accurate for standard operating mode. Eco mode is an additional, optional profile, not a replacement spec.
